# NPCs

The **NPC** actor uses exactly the same rules as a character on a compact sheet.

## Header
Portrait, name, **Role / Species**, alignment, level and **Horror Factor**. With a Horror Factor above 0, the 👻 button posts a Horror Factor card for the players (see Powers → Horror Factor).

## Stats tab
- Attributes (rolled value and total; hover for the bonus).
- Hit Points, S.D.C. (max calculated from Size Level + bonus), S.D.C. Bonus, Natural A.R., Size Level; body armor summary.
- **Combat**: Training, Misc Actions, actions left (with reset), crit range, one-click buttons for every combat roll and maneuver, weapons with attack and damage buttons, and quick **+ Weapon / + Armor**.
- **Saves**: every save, including Coma.
- **Conditions** toggles.

## Details tab
Skills (with rolls), Powers & Abilities (psionics with *Use*, spells with *Cast*/*Practice*), a magic tradition selector with spells today, spell strength and New Day, Gear, and rich-text **Notes**.

Tip: unlinked NPC tokens (the default for NPCs) each track their own damage, actions and conditions.
