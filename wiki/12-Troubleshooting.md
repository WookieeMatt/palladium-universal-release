# Troubleshooting & FAQ

**A Defend or Save button does nothing / says "select your token".**
Select your own token on the canvas (or have the GM assign you a character in User Configuration), then click the button.

**Apply damage says I don't have permission.**
Only the owner of a token can change its health. Players ask the GM to click *Apply* on NPCs.

**The weapon shows "no WP".**
Its *Proficiency* must match a W.P. item's *Weapon Group* (case doesn't matter). Black powder W.P.s match by family instead.

**My skill % looks wrong.**
Hover the % to see base, level, I.Q., education and misc. Check *Level Acquired* on the skill and *Education Bonus %* in the header (Professional skills only).

**Bio-E remaining is negative.**
You've spent more than you have: size increases, Human Features, abilities, natural weapons, psionics, magic training and T.E. change all count.

**Actions don't reset.**
They reset when the Combat Tracker moves to a new round, with a GM connected. Use the ↺ button otherwise.

**A spell has no Cast button.**
It isn't mastered: use *Practice*, or tick *Mastered* on the spell.

**Operate says the device needs recharging.**
Click *Recharge* when the recharge time has passed.

**Dropping a device on a Time Machine didn't add an item.**
That's intended: Time Machines record devices as entries in their zones, not as owned items.

**Updating the system.**
Game Systems tab → *Update*. Your worlds keep their data; older data is migrated automatically when opened.
