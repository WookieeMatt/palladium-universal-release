# Black Powder Weapons

Weapons with the type **Black Powder** get their own panel: **Mechanism** (Arquebus, Matchlock, Wheel lock, Flintlock, Percussion Cap, Cap-and-Ball), **Misfire %**, **Penetration** (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent), **Rifle / musket** (unticked = pistol) and **Deliberately overloaded**. Put the reload rule in **Reload**.

## Weapon Proficiencies
Make a W.P. of kind **Black Powder** and choose its **family**; it applies to that family automatically (no group needed):
| W.P. | Aimed bonus |
|---|---|
| Arquebus and Matchlock | +1 |
| Wheel lock | +2 |
| Flintlock | +2 |
| Percussion Cap (muzzle-loader) | +3 |
| Cap-and-Ball (revolver) | +3 |
| Black Powder (general) | +0, fires everything without penalty, reloads twice as slowly |

All gain +1 at levels 4, 7, 10 and 13. There is no P.P. bonus with guns.

## Firing
- **Aimed**: W.P. bonus. Without a W.P.: no bonus (Arquebus/Matchlock −3), and the careful aim costs **two actions**.
- **Wild**: no bonus with a W.P.; untrained −6 (Arquebus/Matchlock −8).
- **Beyond Range**: the first band past effective range: Arquebus/Matchlock/Wheel lock −8 Strike and half damage; pistols −5 Strike, −4 damage; rifles −5 Strike, −3 damage (the card reminds you of the further bands).
- No bursts. A natural 20 does double damage.

## Misfires
Before the Strike the system rolls for a misfire: the weapon's % + the **weather** (GM setting: humid +5%, rain +15%, downpour or dunking +35%) + 25% if overloaded. The weapon list shows the current chance. On a misfire the **Mishaps table** is rolled:
| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Misfire, Bad Load, Jammed, Fizzle, Handfire | No shot (the card explains the delay) |
| **Overloaded!** | The shot fires with **double damage**, but the gun is destroyed and the shooter takes 1D6 (button) |
| **Explosion!** | The gun is destroyed and the shooter takes 2D6 (button) |

Destroyed guns are unequipped and renamed "(destroyed)".

## Deliberate overloading
Tick *Deliberately overloaded*: +2D6 damage for pistols, +3D6 for rifles, and +25% misfire chance. It needs the weapon's own W.P. (the card warns otherwise).
