## S.D.C. of objects and cover (p.90)
S.D.C. is how much damage an object absorbs before it breaks (0 = broken). It's for the **whole object**: a bullet punching through loses only about **2%** of it (through a brick wall, 200 S.D.C., the damage is −4). A weapon's S.D.C. only matters when someone tries to break it.

**In play:** click the **bricks icon** next to a combatant in the Combat Tracker and pick what they're behind. It lasts the whole combat (the next combat starts without); click again when they move. Firearm, energy and black powder damage applied to them loses the amount below, and the cover wears down by the same amount until it breaks.

## Damage effects & recovery (p.92–93)
- **Bleeding Out:** at **25% of Hit Points** or less a character bleeds: **1 H.P. every minute (4 rounds)** until first aid. *In play:* the **Bleeding Out** condition goes on by itself when damage brings the H.P. that low (and off when healed above it), costs 1 H.P. every 4 rounds in combat, and a **successful First Aid, Paramedic or Medical Doctor** roll gets a **Stop the Bleeding** button (target the bleeding character, then click). The GM can also just remove the condition.
- **Coma and death:** 0 H.P. or less: coma (set automatically). One point below zero per P.E. point can be survived; below −P.E. is death (**Dead** set automatically). Coma lasts one hour per P.E. point without treatment.
- **Save vs Coma** (Combat tab): 16+ on a d20 with the P.E. bonus, 2 of 3 tries, plus treatment: +0 no trained personnel, +1 first aid, +3 paramedic or R.N., +5 doctor without facilities, +7 basic clinic, +9 small hospital, +10 large hospital. One try an hour; failing isn't death unless the coma has outlasted the P.E. in hours. Success: 1 H.P., stable.
- **Healing** (*Heal* button in Health & Damage): no treatment (first aid, poor rest) 1 H.P. a day; non-professional treatment 2 a day; professional 2 the first day, then 4 a day. **S.D.C.:** 5 per hour of rest; none without rest.

## Optional damage side-effects (p.93)
Optional, at the GM's discretion; penalties add up. Setting **Optional Damage Side-Effects**: *Leave it to the GM* (default), *Ask the GM* (a whispered card with a Roll button) or *Roll and apply automatically*. A result becomes an **Injury** item on the sheet carrying the penalties; delete it when it heals.
